# Research Workflow

Consult official documentation, standards, papers, and other primary sources. Reuse
registry/sources.yaml records for the same documents, but reopen references when
reviewing a factual update. Record exact URLs, versions/document editions, dates
actually consulted, supported claims, and any contradictions in design/ research notes.

Distinguish general principles, version-specific behavior, conceptual simulations,
and executed examples. Never invent citations, measurements, or product capabilities.
Do not treat source retrieval or structural validation as technical approval. Keep
technology/comparison version scope in applies_to; put source IDs in article sources,
with their URLs in References. Do not refresh verification dates without actual review.
